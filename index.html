The gap this closes

A loose PDF cannot establish its own provenance.

A search report usually ends up as a loose PDF in someone's downloads folder, carrying no record of what was searched, at which office, through what date, what it cost, or whether the file changed after it arrived. When a facility gets questioned months later, the person who ran the search may no longer be there, and the report cannot establish its own provenance. SearchTrace records the search parameters you supply, pairs them with the receipt, and fixes the file bytes with SHA-256 so the package can be re-checked without re-running anything.

      How a pack is built
      Preserve the official output. Do not reinterpret it.
      
          
      We work only from what you send: the official filing-office search report, the receipt, and your manifest. We transcribe the search parameters exactly as they appear, compute SHA-256 for every file, and assemble a scope summary. We do not re-run the search, contact the filing office, or interpret what the results mean.
      
        
      
        
      
          
      01
      Describe
      Keep the exact debtor name, jurisdiction, filing office, report date, and official through date.
      
          
      02
      Inventory
      Pair the report with its receipt and list every file by role, media type, byte size, and SHA-256.
      
          
      03
      Bound
      State the search logic and exclusions printed by the source. Unknown stays unknown.
      
          
      04
      Re-check
      Recompute hashes later to determine whether the received files are byte-identical.

      Scope boundary
      
        
      An evidence pack is not a lien conclusion.
      
        
      SearchTrace organizes and seals documents you already obtained. It is not a legal opinion, a title report, a certified government search, a lien-clearance conclusion, or a guarantee that no filing exists. We do not validate your debtor-name search logic or name variants, assess whether the filing office's index is complete, determine lien priority, or address fixtures, real-property records, tax or judgment liens, or bankruptcy. Nothing in the pack speaks to filings made after the official through date printed on your report. Credit, perfection, and priority decisions remain yours and your counsel's.
