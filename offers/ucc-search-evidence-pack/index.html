Complete intake

Include seven items.

  1. The official filing-office search report as issued, unaltered.
  2. The filing-office receipt or fee confirmation for that same search.
  3. The manifest JSON exported from the free builder.
  4. The debtor name exactly as submitted to the filing office.
  5. The jurisdiction and filing office searched.
  6. The official through date shown on the report.
  7. The name and email address that should receive the pack.

Do not include Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, cardholder data, credentials, or unrelated customer records.

Scope boundary

Documents in. Evidence pack out.

SearchTrace organizes and seals documents you already obtained. It is not a legal opinion, a title report, a certified government search, a lien-clearance conclusion, or a guarantee that no filing exists.
